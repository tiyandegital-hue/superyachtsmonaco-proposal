FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Buying, selling, chartering, building — and how this brokerage works. If your question is not here, ask it directly.

How much does it cost to charter a superyacht?

+

It depends on the yacht, the season and the destination. Weekly rates are shown on each charter yacht, or marked "Please enquire" where the rate depends on your dates. On top of the rate, running costs (fuel, food, dockage) are paid from an Advance Provisioning Allowance agreed in advance, and VAT depends on the cruising area. Your broker confirms every figure before you sign — nothing is assumed.

What is an APA, and what happens to unspent funds?

+

The Advance Provisioning Allowance is a fund held for the running costs of your charter — fuel, food, drink, dockage and local taxes. The amount is agreed before signing. At the end of the charter the captain accounts for every expense, and any unspent funds are returned to you.

What does "Central Agent" mean?

+

The Central Agent is the broker appointed by the owner to represent a yacht — the one who knows her history, her condition and her real price, and who handles enquiries directly. A boutique house represents a small number of yachts this way, rather than listing hundreds it has never seen.

Can I buy a yacht that is not publicly listed?

+

Yes. Many of the finest yachts change hands without a public listing. Share your brief and we build a private shortlist from on-market and off-market yachts. No names or photographs are shown publicly — the shortlist comes to you, privately.

What does selling through a Central Agent involve?

+

A valuation grounded in comparable sales and the yacht's condition, a pricing strategy agreed with you, professional presentation, and direct outreach to named buyers — including discreet off-market approaches when you ask for them. Your yacht is never published without your say-so.

How long does buying a yacht take?

+

It depends on the yacht and on how specific your brief is. A shortlist can be ready quickly; the survey, sea trial, negotiation and completion take the time they take — and rushing any of them is how buyers regret a purchase. Your broker gives you an honest timeline for your brief, not a standard one.

What is involved in a new build?

+

Brief, design and shipyard selection, build supervision, and delivery. A new build is measured in years, not months, and the budget is agreed per project — there is no standard price for a yacht that does not exist yet. It starts with a conversation about the brief.

How do I enquire about a yacht I have seen here?

+

Use the enquiry form on the yacht's page, or email contact@superyachtsmonaco.com with the yacht's name. The fleet shown on this site is a labelled demo while real Central Agency listings are prepared — the enquiry still reaches the broker directly.

Is my information kept private?

+

Yes. Your name, brief and contact details are used only to prepare your reply and shortlist, and are never shared, sold or published. Off-market enquiries are handled with the same confidentiality as off-market sales. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.
