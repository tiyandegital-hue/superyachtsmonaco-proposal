New Builds

From blank parchment to sovereign wake.

Guided new-build advisory. A new yacht is a long conversation between an owner, a designer and a shipyard — and it starts with the brief, not the brochure. Budgets are agreed per project; there is no standard price for a yacht that does not exist yet.
