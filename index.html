First time chartering?

Chartering, explained.

No jargon, no small print surprises. The structure, plainly — the figures are agreed with your broker before you sign, never assumed.

01 · The rate

What the weekly rate covers

The yacht, her crew and her insurance. That is the rate. Everything else is accounted for separately, and shown to you before signing.

02 · The APA

Fuel, food, dockage

Running costs are paid from an Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA) held for the charter. The amount is agreed in advance, and unspent funds are returned.

03 · Taxes & crew

VAT and gratuity

VAT depends on where the yacht cruises and where the charter begins — your broker confirms the position before signing. Crew gratuity is at your discretion.

Full FAQ

How much does it cost to charter a superyacht?

+

It depends on the yacht, the season and the destination. Weekly rates are shown on each charter yacht, or marked "Please enquire" where the broker confirms the rate for your dates. On top of the rate, running costs are paid from an APA agreed in advance, and VAT depends on the cruising area. Your broker confirms every figure before you sign.

What does "Central Agent" mean?

+

The Central Agent is the broker appointed by the owner to represent a yacht — the one who knows her history, her condition and her real price. A boutique house represents a small number of yachts this way, rather than listing hundreds it has never seen.

Can I buy a yacht that is not publicly listed?

+

Yes. Many of the finest yachts change hands without a public listing. Share your brief and we build a private shortlist from on-market and off-market yachts, with no names or photographs shown publicly.
