What this site uses

This site is deliberately light. It loads no analytics, no advertising trackers and no social-media pixels. The cookies and local storage it uses are:

  • Consent preference — one local-storage entry (sym-cookie-choice) recording whether you accepted or declined cookies, so the banner does not ask you again. Strictly necessary; kept in your browser only.
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Google may set cookies or log requests under its own policy when fonts are fetched.
  • Photography — images loaded from images.unsplash.com. Unsplash may log requests under its own policy when images are fetched.

What we do not use

No analytics cookies. No marketing or advertising cookies. No third-party tracking pixels. No embedded video players, maps or social feeds that set cookies.

The consent banner

On your first visit you are asked to accept or decline. Either choice closes the banner and is remembered. Declining changes nothing about the site — every page, filter and form works the same, because nothing here depends on a cookie.

Changing your choice

Clear your browser's local storage for this site and the banner will ask again on your next visit. You can also control cookies at any time in your browser's settings.
