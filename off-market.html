Off-Market Shortlist

Not every yacht is listed.

Some of the finest yachts never reach the open market. They change hands quietly — owner to buyer, through a broker both sides trust.

What off-market means

No public listing

No name, no photographs, no public record. The yacht is shown privately, to a named buyer, on a brief.

How a shortlist is built

Around your brief

We search on-market and off-market yachts against what you actually want — then approach owners directly, in confidence.

Confidentiality, by default

Yours, and theirs

Your brief is never shared beyond the broker. Owners' details are never shown to you until both sides agree.
