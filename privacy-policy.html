What we collect, and why

SuperYachtsMonaco ("we", "the brokerage") collects only what an enquiry needs: your name, your email address, and whatever you choose to tell us about the yacht, charter or brief you are interested in. A phone number is collected only if you give it. We do not collect payment details, identity documents or financial information through this website.

How we use it

Your details are used for one purpose: to prepare your reply and, where you asked for one, your shortlist. We do not sell, rent or share your details with third parties for marketing. We do not send newsletters unless you separately ask to receive them.

How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as the enquiry is live, and for a reasonable period afterwards so we can follow up — then deleted. You may ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing contact@superyachtsmonaco.com.

Your rights

This website is operated from Monaco. Monaco Law No. 1.565 of 23 December 2024 on the protection of personal data applies, as does the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it is engaged. You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to object to its processing. The supervisory authority in Monaco is the Commission de Contrôle des Informations Nominatives (CCIN).

What loads on this site

This site loads Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and photography hosted on Unsplash (images.unsplash.com). No analytics, advertising trackers or social-media pixels are loaded. Your cookie-consent choice is stored locally in your browser. See our Cookie Policy.

Contact

For any question about your data, email contact@superyachtsmonaco.com or write to SuperYachtsMonaco, Le Monator, 25 Rue Grimaldi, 98000 Monaco.
