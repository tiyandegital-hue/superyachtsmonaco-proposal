About

A boutique brokerage, built on discretion.

SuperYachtsMonaco has been based in Port Hercule, Monaco, since 2008. One office, one principal broker, and a small number of yachts represented properly.

The broker

Jim Evans

Founder & Principal Broker

Jim Evans founded SuperYachtsMonaco in 2008 and remains its principal broker. Every enquiry, every shortlist and every negotiation goes through him — there is no handover to a junior, and no call centre between you and the person who knows the yacht.

The house is deliberately small. A boutique brokerage cannot out-list the big houses, and does not try. It can know fewer yachts, know them well, and answer for them personally — and that is the whole of the offer.

How we work

Four pillars. No noise.

01 · Discretion

Quiet by default

Your name, your brief and your business stay with the broker. Off-market work is handled without any public trace.

02 · Knowledge

Few yachts, known well

As Central Agent we represent a small selection — each yacht's history, condition and price known first-hand.

03 · Honesty

Evidence, not enthusiasm

Prices argued from comparable sales. Yachts shown with their faults as well as their virtues.
