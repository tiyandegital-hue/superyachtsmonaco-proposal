About this website

This website is operated by SuperYachtsMonaco, a boutique yacht brokerage based at Le Monator, 25 Rue Grimaldi, 98000 Monaco. It is a demonstration of the brokerage's fleet browser and enquiry flows. Fleet names, specifications, prices and weekly rates shown on this site are labelled demo placeholders and are not real listings, offers or invitations to treat.

Enquiries

An enquiry made through this website — by form or by email — is not a booking, a reservation, a purchase or a contract. It is a request for information. Availability, price and terms are confirmed by the broker in writing before anything is agreed.

Charter and sale contracts

Charter and sale agreements are made separately, in writing, between the parties. Charter contracts in this market commonly follow the MYBA form; the applicable form and terms are agreed for each transaction and confirmed before signing. Nothing on this website replaces or varies a signed contract.

No obligation

Browsing this site, receiving a shortlist, or requesting a valuation creates no obligation on either side. You may withdraw an enquiry at any time by emailing contact@superyachtsmonaco.com.

Accuracy

We take care to keep this site accurate, but yacht specifications, availability and prices change. Where a figure is marked as a demo placeholder, it is illustrative only. Confirmed figures are given in writing by the broker for a specific yacht and a specific date.

Liability

Nothing on this website is advice. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for loss arising from reliance on demo or illustrative content on this site, or from the unavailability of a yacht shown here.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Principality of Monaco. Disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of Monaco.
